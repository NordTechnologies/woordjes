Learn Dutch vocabulary for the inburgering exam (A1–B2).

Get help

Questions, feedback, or a problem? Email nord_technologies@proton.me. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Inside the app you can also tap Settings → Send logs to developer to share a diagnostic log that helps us fix issues faster.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free? Yes, completely free.

Do I need an account? No. Open the app and start.

Does it work offline? Yes — your words and progress live on your device.

How do I pick or change my level? Take the placement test on first open, or go to Settings → Your level / Reevaluate my level anytime.

How do I reset my progress? Settings → Reset all progress (at the bottom).

How are word levels decided? They are data-driven, based on the NT2Lex CEFR-graded frequency lexicon. See Settings → About & word levels in the app.

Which levels are covered? A1 to B2, with the bulk on A2/B1 (the inburgering range).
