FAQ

Is the app free? Yes, completely free at launch.

Do I need an account? No. Just open the app and start.

Does it work offline? Yes — your words and progress live on your device.

How do I reset my progress? In the app: tap the gear on the Today screen → “Reset all progress”.

Which levels are covered? A1 to B2, with a quick placement test to start you in the right place.

Will there be more words? Yes — the set keeps growing toward 2,500.

Privacy Policy — Woordjes

Last updated: 18 June 2026

Thank you for using Woordjes. We have built this app to respect your privacy completely. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we do not collect any of your data.

1. Who we are

Woordjes is made by Nord Technologies (“we”, “us”). You can reach us anytime at nord_technologies@proton.me.

2. We do not collect your personal data

Woordjes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. We have no servers that receive your information, because the app does not send your information anywhere.

3. Your progress stays on your device

The app remembers your learning progress, your chosen level, and your streak so it can teach you effectively. All of this is stored only on your own device. It never leaves your phone. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

4. No tracking and no advertising

We do not track you across apps or websites. There are no advertisements in Woordjes and no advertising identifiers are used.

5. No third parties

Woordjes does not include third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or social-media tools. No outside company receives data about you through this app.

6. Children’s privacy

Woordjes is intended for a general audience, including adults preparing for the Dutch civic integration exam. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no data from children. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no links that take a user out of the app to unsafe content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, contact us and we will help — though, by design, we hold no such information.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. You remain fully in control of the on-device information at all times, and you can erase it by deleting the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect in a new version of the app.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email nord_technologies@proton.me and we will respond.
